Privacy Policy

YouTube Custom Chat & Danmaku processes live chat exclusively inside your browser and collects no personal data.

What the extension does with data

  • On YouTube watch and live pages, the extension reads the live chat messages that YouTube already shows you (sender name, avatar image address, message text, Super Chat amount, membership notices) so it can display them as a floating chat window and as scrolling comments on the video.
  • This happens entirely inside your browser. Messages are held in memory only for display and are never saved to disk.
  • Your display settings (features on/off, colors, opacity, font sizes, scroll speed, display area, popup language) and the chat window's position and size are saved locally via chrome.storage.local.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not send any data to the developer or to any third party.
  • It has no account system, analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • It does not read pages on any website other than youtube.com.

Sharing & deleting your data

No data is shared, sold, or transferred. Removing the extension deletes its saved settings immediately. You can also reset the chat window position and size from the popup at any time.

Support link & contact

The "Buy the Developer a Coffee" button opens an external website in a new tab only when you click it; that website has its own privacy policy. For questions or issues, open a ticket at github.com/khanhnkq/youtube-chat-enhance/issues.
